# CareWeave 🏥
### Connecting Patient Records, Simplifying Healthcare

CareWeave is a healthcare web application that aims to bring patient information together in one place. Medical records are often scattered across different departments and facilities, making it difficult for doctors to get a complete picture of a patient's medical history.

We are building CareWeave to make that process simpler by connecting patient registration, laboratory work, and doctor consultations through a shared system.

## 💡 What is CareWeave?

CareWeave is built around one simple idea: **a patient's medical history should be connected, accessible to the right people, and easy to understand.**

The platform has three separate portals, each designed for a specific role, while sharing a common backend and database.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Patient Registration:** Register patients and manage their basic information.
- **Appointment Management:** Schedule appointments and manage the patient queue.
- **Laboratory Management:** Handle test orders, sample processing, and lab reports.
- **Doctor Portal:** Access relevant patient history, review reports, and manage consultations.
- **Unified Medical History:** Bring previous reports, diagnoses, and medication records together in one timeline.
- **Record Reconciliation:** Identify possible duplicate records and flag conflicting information for review.
- **Privacy-Focused Access:** Give each user access only to the information needed for their role.

## 🖥️ Three Portals, One Platform

| Portal | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Receptionist | Patient registration, appointments, and check-in |
| Lab Assistant | Laboratory orders, sample tracking, and reports |
| Doctor | Patient history, consultations, and treatment records |

## 🔄 How It Works

1. The receptionist registers the patient and schedules an appointment.
2. The doctor reviews the patient's available medical history and conducts the consultation.
3. If a laboratory test is needed, the doctor creates a test request.
4. The lab assistant processes the request and submits the test results through the laboratory workflow.
5. The authorized doctor reviews the results and updates the consultation record.
6. CareWeave connects the information to the patient's existing history, helping reduce fragmented records.

## 🚀 What Makes CareWeave Different?

We don't want to build just another hospital management system.

Our goal is to make medical records more connected and trustworthy. CareWeave aims to preserve where each record came from, identify potential duplicates, and flag conflicting information instead of silently combining or overwriting records.

This can help healthcare professionals review a patient's history with better context while maintaining appropriate access restrictions.
